Technical Approach & Recommendations

Platform Choice: WordPress + Custom Integration

I recommend WordPress hosted on an EU-based provider (WP Engine EU, Kinsta EU, or similar). WordPress offers:

  • Flexibility: Easily adaptable for your membership, event, and mail campaign needs
  • Cost-effective: Competitive build and hosting costs, no vendor lock-in
  • Maintainability: Your team can manage content with Elementor and Gutenberg (visual builders)
  • Plugin Ecosystem: Mature membership, payment, and email plugins with active communities
  • EU Compliance: Many EU-hosted providers offer GDPR compliance and data residency
